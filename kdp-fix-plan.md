# KDP listing fix plan (8 Sep 2026)

Source of rules: KDP help pages "Update your book details", "Fixing typos on a book title or author name", "Read Sample (Look Inside the Book)".

Rules that decide the plan:
- Title, subtitle and author name are LOCKED 72 hours after publishing. After that only KDP Support can change them, and only for typos ("misspelling of a name"). Capitalisation and rewording officially need a new edition, but Support often does small ones if asked politely. Ask first, republish only if refused.
- Categories, keywords, description and price can be edited any time from the Bookshelf. Each save sends the book back into review (up to 72 hours) during which you cannot edit again. So do ALL edits on a book in ONE session.
- Read Sample appears 9 to 10 BUSINESS days after publishing, 20% of the book. Only books with an ISBN get one. All of yours have ISBNs.

Do the steps in this order. Steps 1 and 2 are one sitting. Step 3 is the long one. Step 4 is three days later.
