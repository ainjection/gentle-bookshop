About this book

Meet Barnaby, a big, friendly blue double-decker bus who lives in the busy little town of Bentwhele and loves nothing more than helping people.

One afternoon, just as Barnaby is feeling tired after a long day, a lady trips and hurts her knee at the zebra crossing. With a kind doctor's help, Barnaby gets a very important job to do: carry her safely all the way to the hospital. Along the way he drives ever so carefully, dreams of being an ambulance, and discovers that being kind makes him feel warm inside.

A warm, gentle story told in big, bold, colourful pictures, with fun sound effects to read out loud. A reassuring way to talk with children about kindness, helping others, being brave, and why hospitals and doctors are nothing to be scared of.

Includes a Meet Barnaby and his Friends section introducing the whole fleet, simple activity pages, and a note for grown-ups.

Who it is for

Children aged 3 to 6 who love buses, trucks and things that go, and any little one with a hospital visit coming up. Big bold pictures and sound effects to shout out loud.
