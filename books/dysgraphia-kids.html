About this book

Built around the gentle trace-and-fade method, every page moves your child from dark letters they trace, to lighter letters, to start dots, to writing on their own. It is the same gradual-release approach occupational therapists use. No crowded pages, no pressure. Just one letter, one clear goal at a time, in large print.

What is inside: warm-up pages that build pencil control with lines, waves, zig-zags and loops. All 26 uppercase and 26 lowercase letters, each with its own full practice page. Numbers 0 to 9 with the same trace-and-fade progression. 24 everyday words and 8 simple sentences to put the letters to work. Open practice pages with the full writing guides for solo writing time.

Start dots on every model letter, spacing dots between letters, and a shaded basement zone so tails like g, j and p know exactly where to go.

Made for real kids. Left-handed friendly: model letters sit at both ends of every row, so a left hand never hides the example while writing. Three-zone writing guides with a dashed midline on every practice row. One skill per page keeps sessions short. Ten relaxed minutes a day is plenty.

Who it is for

Parents of children aged 6 to 9 with dysgraphia or messy handwriting, teachers and teaching assistants, occupational therapy home practice, and homeschool handwriting lessons. Left-handed friendly.
