About this book

This Axolotl Bold and Easy Colouring Book is packed with adorable, simple axolotl scenes drawn in thick, clean, bold outlines. No tiny details, no stress, just relax and fill them in.

Every page features one smiley axolotl in a cute moment: blowing bubbles, riding a turtle, napping on a rock, surfing a leaf, making friends with snails, seahorses and jellyfish, and celebrating the seasons from Halloween and Christmas to Easter, Valentine's and birthdays.

The large print, bold-line style is easy on the eyes and easy on the hands. Each design backs onto a blank page so markers and gel pens will not bleed through, and the 8.5 by 8.5 inch pages are ideal for framing finished art.

Great for stress relief and relaxation, a quiet activity for kids and grandkids, road trips and rainy days, and a sweet gift for birthdays, holidays and animal lovers.

Who it is for

Kids aged 6 and up, teens, adults, seniors and beginners who love cute animals, kawaii art and calm, screen-free downtime. A thoughtful gift for any axolotl lover.
