About this book

138 pages: 101 memories of her girlhood plus a complete 60th party pack, with Birthday Bingo, a table quiz, hidden picture pages and eight retro recipes.

This is not a list of dry dates. It is 101 short memories of her own girlhood and teenage years, each one tied to the year it happened and how old she was. She was 12 at the Saturday roller rink, waiting for the couples skate. She was 14 the summer music videos arrived on cable TV and every teenager in America stayed up to watch.

The toy oven that baked with a light bulb and the big plastic trike. Shrink art, mood rings and a rock in a box sold as a pet. Saturday morning cartoons with songs that taught you grammar, a superheroine spinning into her costume and the way-back seat of the station wagon. Designer jeans, the color-twist cube, giant cans of hairspray and teased bangs, jelly shoes, leg warmers and the mixtape she made off the radio. And the movies she saw at the mall with her friends.

Every memory comes with something to do: a tick box, a quick quiz question or a line to write on, and an Ask Her question so the family can hear the stories behind it. Every page is written for an American girl born in 1967.

Inside you will also find: 1967 at a Glance and a Then and Now page of prices. Hidden picture pages: find the hidden things in a 1970s kitchen, a roller rink, a mall food court, her teenage bedroom and a slumber party. From Mom's Seventies Kitchen: eight recipes to cook for her birthday, from the cheese ball to a chocolate fudge cake. Her Mixtape, the number one song of every year from age 5 to 20. A party pack: six Birthday Bingo cards, a caller's list and a Who Knows Her Best game. 12 word search puzzles with full solutions and a 50 question Birthday Table Quiz. We Survived Somehow, Where Were You When, a letter to her 10 year old self and pages for the family to write in. Pencil drawings throughout.

The perfect 60th birthday gift for her. For a woman turning 60, it is a thoughtful present for mom, a wife, a sister, an aunt or an old friend born in 1967. It is a nostalgia book, a trivia book, a party game book, a cookbook and a keepsake in one, so it is an easy choice when you want a gift for a 60 year old woman who has everything.
