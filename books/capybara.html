About this book

Inside you will find 50 adorable capybara designs drawn in the popular Bold and Easy style: thick clean outlines, big simple shapes, and one design per page. No tiny fiddly details, no stress, just grab your pens and go.

From cosy hot-spring soaks and cafe treats to astronaut capybaras, flower crowns, and seasonal scenes for Halloween, Christmas, Easter and Valentine's, every page is designed to make you smile.

Large 8.5 by 8.5 inch pages, suitable for coloured pencils, gel pens, crayons and markers.

Great for stress relief, mindfulness and screen-free downtime, and a fun gift for anyone who loves the world's calmest animal.
