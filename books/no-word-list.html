About this book

Every Quiz Search in No Word List starts with questions, not a list. Work out the answer, then hunt for it in the grid. When you are done, the letters you never used spell out one more fact.

That is just the first kind of puzzle. Inside you get 100 puzzles in five styles, so no two pages in a row feel the same.

What is inside 40 Quiz Word Searches with no word list and a hidden bonus fact 15 Gap Searches paired with Word Sudoku 15 Code Breakers where every number is a letter 15 Crosswords with straight trivia clues 15 Scrambles with a True or False Trail to finish Three levels: Warm Up, Step Up and Brain Burner Topics include space, animals, science, the human body, planet Earth, history, countries, food, sports and myths A tick off page to track all 100, and every answer at the back
