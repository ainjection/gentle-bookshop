About this book

No colours to choose, no shading to judge, no mess. Every square in the grid holds a number from 1 to 6, and every number is one easy pen mark: a dot, a slash, a cross, a fill. Work through the squares with a single black pen and watch a charming monochrome dog appear, square by square.

Inside this book you will find 100 mystery mosaic puzzles featuring retrievers, pugs, corgis, dachshunds, huskies, spaniels, bulldogs, poodles, terriers and many more. The key is printed beside every puzzle, so there is nothing to remember. Each puzzle has a facing page showing the mark counts and an approximate solving time, and there is a warm-up page at the front to test your pen and practise the six marks. Full solutions are at the back.

Puzzles are printed on the right-hand page only, with a blank back, so your marks never show through. The pages are a large 8.5 by 11 inches with clear grids and easy-to-read numbers.

Why one colour? Mystery mosaics are the calmest kind of puzzle. There is no wrong answer and no artistic skill needed, just a pen, a few quiet minutes, and the small satisfaction of a picture revealing itself.

A relaxing gift for dog lovers, puzzle fans, and anyone who enjoys colour by number, pixel art, or cross-stitch style reveals.
