What is in the pack

  • Two letter charts with the start dot and first move for every small letter, capital and number.
  • Large, medium and small guide lines: the three sizes you step down through in weeks 1 to 3.
  • Line only: just the line to stand on, as on day 17.
  • Notebook size and plain lines: for week 4 and after.
