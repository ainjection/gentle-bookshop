About this book

Soak up the sunshine with 50 cheerful summer scenes. From a busy beach and a splashy water park to a camping trip, a funfair, a farm visit and a big summer festival, every picture hides 12 little objects for young explorers to hunt down.

Each page is a two-in-one activity: first find the 12 hidden objects using the picture key at the bottom, tick them off as you go, then colour in the whole scene.

Fifty unique hand-drawn scenes on big 8.5 by 11 inch pages with clean, bold lines that are easy to colour. Pages are single-sided, which greatly reduces bleed-through.

The perfect companion for the summer holidays. Grab your crayons and start the hunt.

Who it is for

Children aged 4 to 8, and great for the whole family. A screen-free boredom-buster for holidays, road trips and rainy days that builds focus, patience and observation skills.
