About this book

A lion striker, a penguin goalkeeper, a crocodile referee with his little red card, a panda on the ball and a whale who plays in goal. Twenty-four big, friendly football scenes drawn just for small hands.

Every picture has thick bold lines that are easy to stay inside, one picture per page with nothing fiddly or scary, and big open spaces that work for crayons and felt-tips alike.

Pages are single-sided so felt-tips never bleed through onto the next picture.

Whether they are football-mad already or just love cute animals, this is screen-free fun for rainy afternoons, car journeys and quiet time.

Who it is for

Children aged 3 to 8 who are football-mad already or just love cute animals. Screen-free fun for rainy afternoons, car journeys and quiet time, and a good little gift for the football summer.
